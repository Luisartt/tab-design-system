Board meetings

How a TAB board meeting works

Real issues, honest perspective, clear next steps

  1. 1
    Check in
    Each owner shares wins, numbers and what’s on their mind.
  2. 2
    Bring an issue
    One member presents a real challenge or opportunity.
  3. 3
    Get the board’s perspective
    Peers ask hard questions and share what worked for them.
  4. 4
    Commit to action
    Leave with clear next steps, and report back next time.
