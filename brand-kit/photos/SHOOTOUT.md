# TAB Photo Shootout — nano-banana-pro vs seedream-5-pro

Reviewed all 10 candidates (2 models × 5 shots) against `shots.json` briefs and the TAB Design System (real documentary photography, boardroom/work settings, visible diversity, professional-optimistic-direct, no sepia/vintage/script). Every image was inspected full-frame plus 2–4x pixel crops (hands, faces, papers, sticky notes) via a headless-browser crop tool. Dimensions: nano-banana-pro renders at 2752×1536 / 1856×2304; seedream-5-pro at 2560×1440 / 1600×2000.

Scores are 1–5 (5 = best). **Defects** = freedom from AI artifacts (higher is better; 5 = none found).

**Winner: seedream-5-pro.** All 8 people present (an 8th is partially visible behind the front row, confirmed on crop), hands and gesture are anatomically clean, and the left third is genuinely clean, softly-lit wall/window — exactly the negative space the navy veil + white text need. nano-banana-pro is very close (no anatomical defects, good hand/finger detail) but its foreground includes a blurred white door-pillar occupying the left-center of frame, which fights the veil's text zone and wasn't requested in the brief.
Defects noted: none disqualifying on either. Nano's foreground pillar is a composition ding, not an AI artifact.

### Round 2 (art-director override)

The round-1 nano-banana-pro winner was pulled on final review: the four people on the left wear identical light-blue shirts and grey trousers, which reads as a company uniform rather than independent business owners at an invitation-only event, a common AI-uniformity tell. ## Overall model verdict

Both models are usable and, at the pixel level, remarkably clean — across all 10 images, hands/fingers, faces and eyes held up under 2–4x crop inspection, and the only disqualifying defect found anywhere was nano-banana-pro's legible "STRATEGIC PLAN" paper title on shot 02. **seedream-5-pro wins on raw photographic realism** (finer skin micro-texture, more natural imperfections, slightly less "airbrushed/stock" look) and was more careful about not rendering legible fake text on props. **nano-banana-pro wins on composition discipline for TAB's template system** — it consistently left more genuine negative space on the left of 16:9 frames (critical for the navy-veil + white-text overlay) and rendered at a higher native resolution (2752×1536 / 1856×2304 vs. 2560×1440 / 1600×2000). For this batch nano-banana-pro took 3 of 5 shots and seedream-5-pro took 2 of 5; neither model is categorically better, but brief-writers should explicitly call out "leave the left 25–30% of frame clear" for seedream and "no visible/legible text on any prop" for nano-banana-pro on future batches. No shot required a REGENERATE — every shot had at least one acceptable, defect-free candidate.
